# FEC data research and integration handoff

The completed research maps Federal Election Commission (FEC) data and related
public disclosures to a bulk-first acquisition plan. SpicyDocs should preserve
native source files and records; SpicyRegs should publish useful tables,
relationships, filing/legal indexes and explicitly defined financial summaries.
This is an implementation proposal. No ingestion, publication or deployment was
performed by the research task.

## Suggested implementation order

1. **T01–T02: qualify selected routes and prove shared bulk acquisition.** Start
   from the existing [profile boundary](../../src/spicy_docs/releases/profile.py),
   [blob storage](../../src/spicy_docs/storage/blobs.py) and release machinery.
   Prove bounded reads, safe resume, checksums and archive iteration on real files.
2. **T04–T05, T07 and T09: deliver a useful federal slice.** Preserve committee,
   candidate and linkage bulk data plus original filings and amendments. Publish
   source-cited SpicyRegs tables and filing indexes from pinned releases.
3. **T03, T06, T08 and T18: expand and retain history.** Capture short-retention
   sources early, acquire partitioned transactions and raw schedules, and add
   legal/policy documents. Backfill each qualified family without waiting for all
   remaining discovery leads.
4. **T10–T17: reuse the acquisition paths for related sources.** Add IRS, lobbying,
   FARA, union, advertising, ethics and state/local disclosure files. Prioritize
   exact annual exports and unique historical or academic additions.
5. **T19: verify each delivered population and its cost.** Reconcile source-specific
   identities, amendments, representations and coverage. Test bounded memory and
   avoid per-record transfers or repeated full-file reads.

Prefer published compressed bulk files and native manifests, then documented
XML/JSON routes. Prefer XML over equivalent HTML; keep native CSV, Parquet,
database dumps and `.fec` files in their efficient formats. Retain original PDFs
and other unique content separately. Use existing readers and small source
configurations or mappings rather than additional downloader frameworks.

Keep author-derived joins, scores and uncertain identities attributed to their
sources. Define financial rollups by population and amendment rules so overlapping
exports are not summed together. Detailed transaction partitions remain available
through manifests while compact outputs roll into SpicyRegs.

Discovery stopped at the user's requested wrap point. The inventory covers
declared and observed source populations, with explicit gaps; it does not claim
that every public source has been exhausted or every listed body acquired. The
authorized Zyte fallback recovered denied public routes, and remaining failures,
rights conflicts and historical gaps remain attached to the source evidence.
