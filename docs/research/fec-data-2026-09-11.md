# FEC data research and integration handoff

The completed research maps Federal Election Commission (FEC) data and related
public disclosures to a bulk-first acquisition plan. SpicyDocs should preserve
native source files and records; SpicyRegs should publish useful tables,
relationships, filing/legal indexes and explicitly defined financial summaries.
The research task produced the proposal and evidence. Subsequent official FEC
acquisition is implemented in the [FEC reader and CLI](../sources/fec.md); complete
datasets and downstream publication remain separate work.

configurations or mappings rather than additional downloader frameworks.

sources. Define financial rollups by population and amendment rules so overlapping
exports are not summed together. Detailed transaction partitions remain available
through manifests while compact outputs roll into SpicyRegs.
